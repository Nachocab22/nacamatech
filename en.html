01

Data on your device

Dailymalist stores your tasks, habits, and tags locally on your device. The developer does not receive or have access to this information. The current version does not include CloudKit synchronization.

02

Calendar access

Dailymalist requests your permission to access your calendar and display your events within the app. This access is used solely to read and display events, without modifying them or transmitting their contents to the developer or third parties.

You can revoke this permission at any time through your device’s privacy settings.

03

Data collection and use

Dailymalist does not collect personal data off your device, does not send app usage statistics to the developer, and does not use your tasks, habits, tags, or calendar events for advertising or tracking.

04

Data retention and deletion

Local data remains on your device while you use the app. You can remove it by deleting the app and its data from your device. Deleting Dailymalist does not delete events from your calendars.

Any backups made by the operating system depend on your device settings and Apple services. The developer does not have access to these backups.

05

Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated if the app’s functionality or data practices change. The update date will appear at the top of this page.
